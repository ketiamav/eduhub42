# EduHub42 Website

## Goal
Build a polished, responsive education ecosystem website that positions EduHub42 as an Education Development Enterprise across academic development, education access, and career readiness.

## Pages and navigation
- Build a rich home page with the banner-style opening, About, three connected pillars, four-step journey, booking, testimonials, resources, partnerships, contact, and footer.
- Add dedicated pages for About, Pillars, Booking, Careers, Contact, Resources, and six starter article pages so primary navigation and shareable content have real destinations.
- Keep Careers in the navigation as requested while also giving it a prominent final/footer entry point.
- Add a responsive sticky header that transitions from the full-width brand banner into a compact navigation bar.
- Add the persistent WhatsApp Channel control on every page, with a compact mobile treatment.

## Brand and visual system
- Use deep navy, teal/turquoise, white, and restrained light-grey surfaces with semantic design tokens.
- Use the uploaded official logo unchanged once provided; no recreation or boxed logo treatment.
- Create a distinctive abstract education/pathway visual language using connected routes, progression marks, editorial typography, generous spacing, restrained cards, and purposeful motion.
- Tell one visual story throughout: Learn → Grow → Succeed.

## Forms and interactions
- Add separate validated booking flows for parents/guardians and learners/students.
- Add a validated general enquiry form with partnership subject prefill.
- Add a dedicated careers page with three roles, validated application forms, CV upload, and optional supporting documents.
- Add clear loading, error, and confirmation states for every form.
- Send submissions by branded email to info@eduhub42.co.za after the EduHub42 email domain is configured and verified.

## Content and discoverability
- Use the supplied copy, contact details, social links, and calls to action.
- Add understated editable testimonial placeholders and six structured starter resource articles.
- Give every page unique titles, descriptions, social metadata, canonical links, semantic headings, and accessible image descriptions.
- Add structured data where appropriate and keep article pages ready for future content expansion.

## Accessibility and quality
- Ensure keyboard-friendly menus, dialogs, forms, focus states, labels, error messages, and large mobile touch targets.
- Prevent horizontal overflow and verify desktop and mobile layouts, sticky navigation, links, dialogs, uploads, form validation, and success states.
- Keep motion subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Inputs and external setup
- **Logo:** Await the official EduHub42 logo upload before final visual verification.
- **Email:** Set up a sender domain owned by EduHub42. Email delivery begins once its DNS verification is complete.
- **Form data:** Submission emails will contain entered details and attached documents; no separate database or admin dashboard is included.
